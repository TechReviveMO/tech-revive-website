Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do you have a walk-in shop?

No walk-in storefront. Tech Revive provides scheduled drop-off repairs in St. Joseph. We share the agreed drop-off location privately after confirming your appointment.

Can you come to me for a repair?

Mobile repair may be available for eligible devices, depending on distance, schedule, and safe working conditions. We confirm any additional travel fee before you book.

How long does a screen repair take?

Many standard front-screen repairs take around 30 minutes once the correct part is on hand. Some repairs take longer, and we will give you a realistic estimate for your phone.

Do you keep every screen in stock?

No. We generally order the correct part for your specific phone. We'll confirm the estimated delivery time before ordering.

Is a deposit required?

For special-order parts, a deposit may be required and goes toward your repair total. We'll explain the amount and cancellation/refund terms before you pay.

What if I have phone insurance?

We recommend checking your deductible and coverage first. Your insurance option may be less expensive depending on your plan.

Will my phone be waterproof after a repair?

We cannot guarantee the original factory water resistance after a phone has been opened.

Are your screens genuine Apple parts?

Our standard replacement options may be aftermarket parts. We'll explain the selected grade, compatibility, and any known feature limitations before you approve the repair.
